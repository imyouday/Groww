**ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme)** is a mutual fund category that qualifies for tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, up to ₹1.5 lakh per financial year.

ELSS has a **mandatory 3-year lock-in period** from the date of each investment (each SIP installment locks for 3 years). You cannot redeem or switch units before 3 years. ELSS invests predominantly in equities (minimum 80%). Unit values move with the market. Long-term capital gains (LTCG) above ₹1.25 lakh per year are taxed at 12.5% (post-Budget 2024). Verify current tax rates on the official Income Tax portal.
