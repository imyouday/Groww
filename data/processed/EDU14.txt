**KYC (Know Your Customer)** is mandatory before investing in any mutual fund. It verifies your identity and address using PAN, Aadhaar, and in-person verification (IPV) or video KYC. Once done with a SEBI-registered KRA (KYC Registration Agency), it is valid across all fund houses.

**Nominee** is a person you designate to receive your mutual fund units in case of your demise. Up to 3 nominees can be added with percentage allocation. Nomination is optional but highly recommended — it simplifies transmission. You can add/update nominees online via the AMC or RTA portal. Joint holders are not nominees; the first holder's nominee applies.
