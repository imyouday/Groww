SEBI mandates that every mutual fund scheme fit into one standard category. Main equity categories:

- **Large Cap**: Minimum 80% in large-cap stocks (top 100 by market cap)
- **Mid Cap**: Minimum 65% in mid-cap stocks (101st–250th)
- **Small Cap**: Minimum 65% in small-cap stocks (251st onward)
- **Flexi Cap**: Minimum 65% in equity, free to move across market caps
- **Multi Cap**: Minimum 75% in equity, with at least 25% each in large/mid/small
- **ELSS**: Minimum 80% in equity, 3-year lock-in, tax benefit under 80C
- **Hybrid / Balanced Advantage**: Mix of equity and debt, dynamic allocation
- **Debt Categories**: Liquid, Ultra Short, Low Duration, Money Market, Short Duration, Medium, Medium to Long, Long Duration, Corporate Bond, Credit Risk, Banking & PSU, Gilt, Floater
- **Index Funds / ETFs**: Replicate a specific index (NIFTY 50, SENSEX, etc.)
- **FoF (Fund of Funds)**: Invests in other mutual fund schemes (domestic or overseas)

The category defines the scheme's investment universe and risk profile.
