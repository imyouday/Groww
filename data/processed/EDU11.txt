A **Benchmark** is a market index (e.g., NIFTY 50 TRI, NIFTY Smallcap 250 TRI, CRISIL Liquid Fund Index) against which a mutual fund's performance is measured.

The fund manager aims to beat the benchmark over the long term. Benchmark choice reflects the scheme's category and investment universe. Total Return Index (TRI) versions are used (including dividends). Comparing a fund to its benchmark helps assess skill. A fund consistently underperforming its benchmark may indicate issues. However, short-term underperformance is normal even for good funds.
