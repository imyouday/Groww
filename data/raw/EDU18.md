A **Capital Gains Statement** summarizes your realized gains/losses for a financial year, needed for tax filing.

**Where to get it:**
1. **RTA Portals** (CAMS: camsonline.com, KFintech: kfintech.com) — covers all folios across fund houses serviced by that RTA
2. **AMC Website / App** — log in and download from "Statements" or "Tax Center"
3. **Consolidated Account Statement (CAS)** — monthly email from NSDL/CDSL, or download from CAMS/KFintech
4. **MF Central (mfcentral.com)** — single view across all RTAs

**Steps (typical):**
- Log in with PAN/OTP
- Select "Capital Gains Statement" or "Tax Statement"
- Choose financial year
- Download PDF/Excel

The statement shows: scheme name, folio, purchase date/NAV, sale date/NAV, units sold, gain/loss amount, holding period (STCG/LTCG), and applicable tax. Verify with your broker or tax advisor before filing.
