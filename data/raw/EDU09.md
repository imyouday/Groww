Mutual fund schemes offer two main options: **Growth** and **IDCW (Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal, formerly Dividend)**.

- **Growth Option**: All profits stay invested. NAV grows over time. You realize gains only when you redeem. Tax-efficient for long-term investors (LTCG taxed only on redemption).
- **IDCW Option**: The fund distributes part of its gains as payouts at the AMC's discretion. NAV falls by the payout amount. Payouts are taxed at your slab rate as income. Reinvestment option (IDCW Reinvestment) buys more units with the payout.

For wealth creation, Growth is usually more tax-efficient. IDCW suits those needing periodic income.
