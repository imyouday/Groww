**Mutual fund taxation** depends on the fund type (equity vs. debt) and holding period. Tax rates below are as of FY 2024–25 (Budget 2024). **Verify current rates on the official Income Tax portal.**

**Equity-Oriented Funds** (≥65% equity):
- STCG (≤12 months): 20%
- LTCG (>12 months): 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh per financial year (no indexation)

**Debt-Oriented Funds** (<65% equity):
- STCG (≤24 months): Taxed at your slab rate
- LTCG (>24 months): 12.5% without indexation (post-Budget 2024)

**Hybrid Funds** — taxed based on actual equity allocation (check SID/factsheet).

**Dividends/IDCW payouts** — taxed at your slab rate (TDS 10% if >₹5,000/year).

**STT (Securities Transaction Tax)** applies on equity fund redemptions (0.001%).

**Indexation benefit** removed for debt funds (Budget 2024). Specified mutual funds (certain FoFs, debt-heavy) have different rules — check the SID.

**Always verify tax treatment on the official Income Tax portal or with a chartered accountant before filing.**
