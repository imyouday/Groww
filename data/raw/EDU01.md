**NAV (Net Asset Value)** is the per-unit price of a mutual fund scheme.

NAV is calculated daily by dividing the total value of the fund's assets (stocks, bonds, cash) minus liabilities by the number of units outstanding. It reflects the market value of the underlying portfolio. You buy or sell units at the NAV of the day (plus any entry/exit load). NAV is not a measure of whether a fund is cheap or expensive, and the system does not compare NAVs or compute returns.
