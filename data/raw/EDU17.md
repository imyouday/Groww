**Portfolio Turnover Ratio** measures how frequently a fund buys/sells securities. High turnover (>100%) means active trading, higher transaction costs, and potential tax implications. Low turnover (<20%) indicates a buy-and-hold approach. Index funds typically have very low turnover.

**Tracking Error** measures how closely an index fund/ETF follows its benchmark. High tracking error means the fund deviates from the index due to costs, cash drag, or sampling.

**Alpha** is the return over the benchmark, adjusted for risk (beta). The published figure is a measure of benchmark-relative movement, not a recommendation.

**Beta** measures sensitivity to market movements. Beta = 1 moves with market; >1 amplifies market moves; <1 dampens them. Debt funds have beta near 0.

**Sharpe Ratio** = (Fund Return - Risk-Free Rate) / Standard Deviation. It is a risk-adjusted measure of return per unit of volatility.

These metrics are disclosed in monthly factsheets and annual reports. This assistant does not compute or compare them; read the published figures on the official factsheet.
