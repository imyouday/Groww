**SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)** lets you invest a fixed amount at regular intervals (e.g., monthly). **Lump sum** means investing a large amount at once.

SIP averages your purchase price over time (rupee cost averaging), reducing the impact of market volatility. It builds discipline and suits salaried investors. Lump sum can outperform if invested at a market low, but timing the market is difficult. For most retail investors, SIP is the recommended approach for equity funds. You can also combine both: start with a lump sum and continue with SIP.
