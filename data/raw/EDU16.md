**SID (Scheme Information Document)** is the comprehensive legal document for a mutual fund scheme, filed with SEBI. It contains: investment objective, asset allocation, risk factors, fees (TER, exit load), fund manager details, benchmark, portfolio constraints, taxation, accounting policies, and more. The SID is the authoritative source for all scheme facts.

**KIM (Key Information Memorandum)** is a concise summary of the SID, designed for quick reading. It covers: scheme type, investment objective, asset allocation, riskometer, exit load, expense ratio, minimum investment, fund manager, benchmark, and performance snapshot. KIM is updated annually or on material changes.

Both documents are available on the AMC website, distributor platforms, and the SEBI portal. Always refer to the latest SID/KIM before investing.
