Every mutual fund scheme offers two plans: **Direct** and **Regular**.

- **Direct Plan**: Units are bought directly from the AMC, with no distributor. The expense ratio (TER) excludes distributor commission.
- **Regular Plan**: Units are bought through a distributor or broker, so the TER includes distributor commission.

The portfolio, fund manager, and investment objective are identical. The only difference is the cost structure. Switching from Regular to Direct is treated as a redemption, so exit load and capital gains tax may apply. Which plan is preferable depends on your own situation, so compare the two published expense ratios and check them with your advisor.
