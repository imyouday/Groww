**STP (Systematic Transfer Plan)** automatically transfers a fixed amount from one mutual fund scheme (usually a liquid or debt fund) to another (usually an equity fund) at regular intervals. It helps deploy a lump sum gradually into equity, reducing timing risk.

**SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan)** lets you withdraw a fixed amount from your mutual fund investment at regular intervals (e.g., monthly). It provides regular income while keeping the remaining amount invested. SWP is often used by retirees. Both STP and SWP are automated and execute at the prevailing NAV. Exit load and tax implications apply based on the source scheme and holding period.
