**AUM (Assets Under Management)** is the total market value of all investments a mutual fund scheme manages on behalf of its investors.

AUM changes daily with market movements and investor flows (inflows from new purchases/SIPs, outflows from redemptions). A large AUM can indicate investor confidence and economies of scale (lower expense ratio), but very large AUM in small-cap funds may limit flexibility. AUM alone does not predict performance.
